# Executive proposal inspection checklist

Use one row per user requirement. Mark a row `passed` only when the final rendered file contains the requirement and the source builder can reproduce it. Mark conditional rows `not applicable` with a brief reason instead of forcing them into an unrelated industry.

| Check | Applies when | Evidence to locate | Typical failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience and decision | Always | Decision maker, target result, decision rights, and acceptance standard | Starts with a feature list or addresses only a project manager |
| Discovery | Owner interview or diagnosis is in scope | Why now, target operating number, red line, unknowns, and reciprocal inputs | Jumps from a surface pain directly to a full solution |
| Scope | Always | Phase, duration, channels or systems, modules, quantities or explicit no-fixed-quantity decision | Old phase, price, or quantity survives in a footer or note |
| Numbered reference | The user points to a numbered item or version | The item is resolved against the named source and preserved or deliberately merged | A section number is guessed or a required item disappears |
| Business flow | Always | Editable loop with investment, operating actions, evidence, and executive decision | Boxes show features but no business result |
| Primary workflow | Always | Sources or records, IDs, rules, agent steps, exceptions, permissions, outputs, and data return | “AI automation” appears with no inputs, state, or failure path |
| Development path | Always | Time-boxed plan with owner gates and acceptance evidence | Timeline lists activity but no decision or proof |
| Roles and adoption | Always | Current role, future role, approval rights, training, handover, and error owner | System delivery ignores who uses it or carries responsibility |
| Content IP | Content or channel-IP work is in scope | Audience, voice, visual grammar, training corpus, reference boundary, and responsibility split | References are presented as client results or every account shares one voice |
| Workbench | A local control surface or multi-system view is in scope | Operating views, canonical IDs, permissions, decisions, and task states | A database is renamed as a workbench without useful views |
| Growth | Acquisition or adjacent-market work is in scope | Overlap criteria, entry scene, hook, offer, evidence, and owner | Generic industry list with no customer overlap or action |
| Operating ledger | Always | Saved effort is tied to reduced cost, avoided hiring, capacity, cash speed, waste reduction, or sales | Hours saved are presented as cash with no conversion path |
| Organization ledger | Always | Winners, likely resistance, changed authority, new review work, adoption owner, and evidence | Proposal ignores incentives and assumes usage |
| Risk and capability ledger | Always | Failure modes, limits, approval points, error owner, rollback, and stop conditions | Limits are hidden or “human review” has no named owner |
| Economics | Pricing or ROI is in scope | Benchmark source, comparable roles, coefficients, period, phase total, included allowances, exclusions, first-year and later-year formulas | Tools are double-counted or one-time and annual costs are mixed |
| 24 and 48-hour follow-up | Diagnosis-to-proposal follow-up is requested | Owner, recipient, authorized input, deliverable, due time, channel, and proof of delivery | A follow-up is promised but has no owner or is skipped silently |
| Executive proof | Always | Live end-to-end demonstrations and a decision pack tied to success standards | Acceptance is only an asset count |
| Sources | External facts, brands, or cases inform the proposal | Official or current sources in notes and Word; internal cases labeled | An invented result, unlabeled reference, or stale URL remains |
| Visual QA | PPT or Word is delivered | Every rendered page and slide inspected after the last edit | Only text extraction or a contact sheet was checked |

State that every check passed only after the final render, content scan, and arithmetic scan.

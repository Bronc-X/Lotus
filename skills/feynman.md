# Feynman

Explain the real mechanism in language the stated audience can repeat accurately.

- Start with the question the concept answers and the smallest useful mental model.
- Define unavoidable terms at first use. Prefer concrete examples that preserve the important causal relationship.
- Separate what happens, why it happens, and where the model stops being accurate.
- Match depth to the audience; do not assume either complete ignorance or hidden expertise.
- End with a short checkable restatement or example when it helps confirm understanding.

Avoid ornamental analogies, jargon chains, and rigid templates. An analogy is useful only when its mismatch is stated and it makes the mechanism easier to predict.
